Who can win

Every ticket played during the week counts: 1 Loto ticket = 1 chance, 1 Raffle ticket = 1 chance and 1 Treasure dig = 1 chance. A ticket counts in the week it is bought: the week runs from the Sunday Raffle draw to the next Sunday Raffle draw.

Every ticket and dig feeds the airdrop pot right away. The amount shown is exact at any time: it is what the airdrop contract already holds for this week.

The draw

Every Monday at 19:00 UTC, the day after the big Sunday raffle, 3 different wallets are drawn, each with chances proportional to its tickets:

WinnerPrize
1st50% of the airdrop
2nd30% of the airdrop
3rd20% of the airdrop

Prizes are sent directly to the winners' wallets: nothing to claim. With fewer than 3 players, the unawarded prizes roll over to the next airdrop.

Randomness, and how to verify it

After the week closes, the list of players is computed from the public data of both games and stored in the contract. Only then do two independent parties reveal their secrets (each one extends a chain committed in advance). The winners are computed from both secrets and the list's fingerprint. The “Verify” button rebuilds the list from the games and redoes the whole draw in your browser.

If the draw isn't completed within 6 hours, it is cancelled and the whole week (players and pot) is carried into the next airdrop.
