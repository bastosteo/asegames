How it works

Own a racehorse. Buy up to 3 horses. The price sets the class: Outsider 10 ASE, Hopeful 12, Standard 15, Challenger 20, Challenger+ 25, Elite 35. A better class starts with a better rating.

Take care of it. Feed it once a day (1 ASE): a hungry horse runs slower, and a horse fed twice within 24 h gets bloated and slower too. Train it (2 ASE) for a boost in its next race, but training it twice within 24 h before a race risks an injury (it then rests a few days).

One race a day at 19:00 (Paris). Enter your horse (5 ASE) until 18:00. Empty stalls are filled with house horses. The owner of the winner takes 90% of the owners' pot (the house keeps 10%). If a house horse wins, the pot is carried over to the next race.

Spectator bets. Once the runners are known (from 18:00), anyone can bet: Win, Exacta, Quinella, Trifecta, Trifecta any order. Pool betting: 15% goes to the house, the rest is shared between the winning bets. Winnings are paid automatically right after the draw.

The draw. Two independent croupiers each seal a secret before the race and reveal it after the start: together they decide the finishing order, the same in the stable and the bets contracts. Nobody, not even the admin, can choose the winner. Each horse's rating is shown; exact chances stay hidden.

Falls. Rarely a horse falls: it finishes last and is injured for a few days. An overtrained horse falls more often.

Safety. If a croupier fails to reveal within an hour, the race is cancelled: entries are refunded automatically and bets can be refunded from "My bets".
