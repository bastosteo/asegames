How it works

5 races a day at 18:00, 19:00, 20:00, 21:00 and 22:00 (Paris time). For each race, 6 horses out of the 18 in the stable run on one of 4 kinds of going: Firm, Soft, Heavy or Sand. Each horse's chance depends on its form, its rest and whether the going suits it.

Bets close when the race starts. Win: 2 to 10 ASE. Exacta, Quinella, Trifecta and Trifecta any order: 1 to 5 ASE.

Pool betting. All the stakes of a race make one pot. 15% goes to the house, the rest is shared between the winning bets according to their stake and how unlikely the bet was. A winning bet always gets at least its stake back. Winnings are sent to your wallet automatically, right after the draw. If nobody wins, the pot is carried over to the next race.

The draw. Two independent croupiers each seal a secret before bets open. After the start, both reveal their secret: together they decide the finishing order in the contract, so nobody (not even the admin) can choose the winner. The 3D race then replays that result, the same for everyone.

Falls. Rarely (about once every 50 races) a horse falls, more often when it is out of form and not on its favourite going. A horse that falls finishes last, bets on it are lost, and it rests for 3 days.

Safety. If a croupier fails to reveal within an hour, the race is cancelled and every player can get all their stakes back from "My bets".

Contract: see the explorer (Asentum testnet). Testnet ASE has no monetary value.
