The game

Buy tickets during the week: one ticket costs 5 ASE, and each ticket is one chance to win. You can hold up to 10 tickets per wallet for each raffle. The draw takes place every Sunday at 19:00 UTC; sales close 10 minutes before.

The prizes

WinnerPrize
1st50% of the prize pot
2nd30% of the prize pot
3rd20% of the prize pot

Three different wallets win every week: a wallet can't win twice in the same draw.

If fewer than 3 wallets took part when sales close, the raffle is extended by one week: your tickets stay valid and the pot keeps growing.

The prize pot is 85% of the week's sales. Each ticket is also one chance in the weekly ASE Airdrop.

Claiming: winners click Claim in “My tickets” to receive their prize. Prizes never expire.

Randomness, and how to verify it

Two independent parties each prepared a long chain of secrets, and only the tip of each chain is stored in the contract. After sales close, both reveal the week's secret; the winning tickets are then computed from both secrets and the fingerprint of every purchase. The “Verify” button redoes the whole computation in your browser.

If a secret isn't revealed

If both secrets aren't published within 2 hours after sales close, the raffle is cancelled and every player gets a 100% refund.

Asentum testnet: testnet ASE has no monetary value.
