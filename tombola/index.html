The game

One ticket costs 5 ASE, and each ticket is one chance to win. You can hold up to 10 tickets per wallet for each raffle. Draws take place every Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday at 19:00 UTC; sales close 10 minutes before, and the winners are revealed at the draw time.

The prizes

Up to 30 winning tickets in every draw:

WinnerPrize
Lucky lotsUp to 27 lots of 100 ASE, using at most 40% of the pot
1st50% of the rest of the pot
2nd30% of the rest of the pot
3rd20% of the rest of the pot

Example: with a 9,700 ASE pot, 27 lucky lots of 100 ASE, then 3,500 / 2,100 / 1,400 ASE for the top 3. With a 3,000 ASE pot, 12 lucky lots (40% of the pot), then 900 / 540 / 360 ASE.

Each ticket can win only once, but a player with several tickets can win several prizes. If there are fewer tickets than prizes, the prizes without a ticket are added to the next raffle. A raffle with no ticket at all simply moves to the next draw.

The prize pot grows with every ticket sold, plus any prizes carried over. Each ticket is also one chance in the weekly ASE Airdrop.

Claiming: winners click Claim in “My tickets” to receive their prize. Prizes never expire.

Buy tickets during the week: one ticket costs 2 ASE, and each ticket is one chance to win. You can hold up to 10 tickets per wallet for each raffle. The draw takes place every Sunday at 19:00 UTC; sales close 10 minutes before, and the winners are revealed at the draw time.

The prizes

WinnerPrize
1st50% of the prize pot
2nd30% of the prize pot
3rd20% of the prize pot

Three different wallets win every week: a wallet can't win twice in the same draw.

The raffle is always drawn, even with fewer than 3 wallets. With 2 wallets, the 1st and 2nd prizes are drawn as usual and the 3rd prize is added to next week's pot; with 1 wallet, it wins the 1st prize and the rest goes to next week. A raffle with no ticket at all simply moves to the next week.

The prize pot grows with every ticket sold, plus any prizes carried over from the previous raffle. Each ticket is also one chance in the weekly ASE Airdrop.

Claiming: winners click Claim in “My tickets” to receive their prize. Prizes never expire.

No cancellations, no refunds

A ticket can't be cancelled or refunded. If the draw runs late, the raffle simply waits: the winners are drawn as soon as possible.

Contract and admin powers

Contract: see the explorer (Asentum testnet). Every ticket, draw and payment is public on chain.

What the admin can do: pause ticket sales (draws and prize claims keep working), change the ticket price, draw days and time and ticket limits for the next raffles, change the size and number of lucky lots for the next raffles (never for a raffle already open), and change the wallets that receive the game fees. What the admin cannot do: move, freeze or take players' funds, the prize pot or unclaimed prizes.

Asentum testnet: testnet ASE has no monetary value.
