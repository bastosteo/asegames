The game

A treasure is hidden somewhere on a 20 × 20 map (400 cells). Every player digs the same map. One dig costs 0.5 ASE, with up to 5 digs per wallet on each map. A cell can only be dug once, and every answer stays visible to everyone.

🔥 HotThe treasure is within 3 cells (diagonals included)
❄️ ColdThe treasure is further away

The treasure

The treasure is 85% of all the digs on the map, up to the winning dig. It is sent to the finder's wallet (a Claim button appears right away, and the game sends it automatically within a few minutes otherwise). A new map starts a few minutes later. Digs confirmed just after the treasure was found are refunded. Each dig is also one chance in the weekly ASE Airdrop.

Why you can trust the map

Before the first dig, the contract stores a fingerprint (a Merkle root) that locks the 400 answers of the map. Nobody can move the treasure or change an answer afterwards. Every answer comes with its proof, checked by your browser against that fingerprint. When a map is over, the whole map is revealed and the “Verify” button checks every answer.

If no treasure is found within 30 days, the map is cancelled and every dig is refunded.
